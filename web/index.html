The city under the city

A palimpsest is a page scraped clean and written over. Colonial Calcutta is one: Thacker's directories, survey sheets, studio photographs and the memories of today's shopkeepers are all layers on the same streets. Click a building and see who held it in 1897, 1915 or 1940 — and what the archive chose not to record.

One Address
