The city under the city.

Click any building on a colonial Calcutta street — Chowringhee in 1897, Clive in 1915, Bowbazar in 1940 — and see who occupied it, what shop stood there, and what the archive chose not to record.

Open the map Read the argument

A palimpsest is a page scraped clean and written over. This project treats colonial Calcutta the same way: Thacker's directories, Survey of India sheets, photos from Bourne & Shepherd, and the memories of present-day shopkeepers are all layers over the same streets. We show each layer, and the gaps between them.
