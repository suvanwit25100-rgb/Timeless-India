The colonial archive is uneven. White Town addresses were recorded house by house; most Indian residents and shops in the north of the city were not. Fuzzy and unmatched rows are candidates, not certain fact. The base map is a modern street plan, not a colonial survey; pin positions are approximate until Simms' 1847–49 survey is georeferenced.
